🧠 Our Project

Welcome to our Social Graphs project! We investigate Marvel Comics as a network of characters connected through relationships and Wikipedia links.

Using Python, NetworkX, graph theory and NLP , we transform the Marvel universe into data that can be explored and analyzed.

Instead of simply asking who the most famous characters are, we ask: Who is the most connected? Which characters form communities? And can text similarity help us identify a mystery character?
