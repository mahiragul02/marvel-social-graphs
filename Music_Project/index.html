Beyond Similarity: Mapping the Semantic and Biographical Networks of 500 Musicians

We read the Wikipedia biographies of historically prominent musicians and link them by the words they use and the meaning they carry, then ask which groups appear.

Research question 1To what extent do lexical and semantic representations of musicians’ Wikipedia biographies produce similar network communities, and what biographical themes distinguish those communities?

Method

Data

497 musicians from the Historical Popularity Index top 500, with cleaned English Wikipedia biographies: references, links and discographies removed.

Representations

Lexical: TF-IDF word weights, with and without proper nouns. Semantic: sentence embeddings of text chunks, averaged per biography.

Networks

Cosine similarity between biographies, then each musician is linked to the K most similar musicians.

Communities

Louvain detection repeated over many random seeds. Partitions compared with ARI and NMI against a permutation baseline.
