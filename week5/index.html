Marvel's Wikipedia pages were written by many hands, so you would expect different words. This page hunts for the opposite: runs of eight or more words that appear, unchanged, on more than one page.

CASE STUDY · THE SAME EIGHT WORDS

Most phrases are one-offs, a few are everywhere

Every run of 8 consecutive words (an 8-gram) was collected from all 303 pages. This counts the 8-grams that appear on more than one page, by how many pages share them.

Who copies whom

Each dot is a character and each line joins two pages that share many 8-grams between only a few pages (template phrases found on 5+ pages are left out). Line thickness is the number of shared 8-grams. Drag a dot, click a dot to isolate its lines, or click a line to read the copied text.
