Similarity

Compare pages using cosine similarity.

Cosine Similarity
similarity(A,B) = (A · B) / (||A|| ||B||)
A value closer to 1 indicates more similar word distributions, while a value closer to 0 indicates little textual overlap.

06 · Interpretation

How should we interpret the results?

Similar vocabulary can indicate similar roles

Characters who appear in similar stories, teams, locations or publication contexts may naturally have Wikipedia pages containing similar vocabulary.

Text similarity is different from graph similarity

Two characters can be textually similar without necessarily having the same number of network connections. Text analysis therefore provides a different perspective on the Marvel universe.

Shared contexts matter

Characters appearing in the same teams, storylines or locations may develop similar textual descriptions even when their individual personalities are very different.

Similarity is not causality

A high cosine similarity does not mean that one character influences another. It simply means their pages contain similar patterns of words.

07 · Limitations

What are the limitations?

Bag of Words ignores word order "Spider-Man defeats Venom" and other arrangements of the same words can appear equivalent at the Bag-of-Words level.
Common vocabulary can dominate Marvel-related terms can appear across many pages and contribute to similarity.
Wikipedia pages vary in length Some characters have much longer and more detailed pages than others.
Similarity is not semantic understanding The method compares word distributions rather than understanding the meaning of the text.

09 · Conclusion

From Connections to Content

Previous weeks examined the Marvel universe as a network of characters and relationships. This week adds another perspective by analysing the text used to describe those characters.

Using Bag-of-Words representations and cosine similarity, we can identify characters whose Wikipedia pages have similar textual patterns. The interactive Text Detective turns this analysis into an exploratory game, allowing us to investigate textual relationships directly.
