Week 3 — The Surprising Bridge

Who has more influence than their popularity suggests?

The Question

A character's degree (how many others link to/from them) measures popularity. Their betweenness centrality measures how often they sit on the shortest path between other characters — i.e. how much of a "bridge" they are.

Highly popular characters are expected to have high betweenness too — they're hubs. The surprising cases are characters with modest degree but disproportionately high betweenness: minor characters who quietly connect two otherwise-separate corners of the Marvel universe.

So Who Is It, and What Do They Bridge?

Ahura appears to function as a connector between two distinct communities in the Marvel character network: the Inhuman community and the broader superhero community. His connections to Black Bolt, Medusa, and other members of the Inhuman royal family place him firmly within the Inhuman cluster, while his relationships with characters outside this group create links to a more diverse set of Marvel characters. This makes Ahura relevant from a network perspective because he can act as a bridge between otherwise densely connected communities. His position is particularly notable given his relatively small number of direct connections compared with more central characters. Rather than being important because of high degree centrality, his significance may come from his position between communities, potentially giving him higher betweenness centrality and making him a useful intermediary between the two clusters.
