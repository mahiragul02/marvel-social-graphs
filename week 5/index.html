01 — Research Question

Can Wikipedia text reveal hidden similarities?

In the previous weeks, we investigated Marvel characters as a social network, where characters were connected through relationships in the network. In Week 5, we approach the Marvel universe from a different perspective: the text describing each character.

We use Marvel Wikipedia pages as a collection of textual documents and compare their content using Natural Language Processing (NLP). The goal is to determine which characters have the most similar textual descriptions.

02 — Dataset

From Marvel pages to text data

The analysis uses a collection of Marvel character Wikipedia pages. Each page is treated as an individual document. Before calculating similarity, the raw HTML content is cleaned and converted into a representation based on word frequencies.

1

Wikipedia Pages

Character pages are collected in a ZIP archive. The pages provide the textual information used for the analysis.

2

Text Cleaning

HTML tags, scripts, styles and punctuation are removed. Words are converted to lowercase and common stopwords are excluded.

3

Word Frequencies

Each document is represented by a collection of words and their frequencies. This is our Bag-of-Words representation.

03 — Method

How do we measure textual similarity?

Two character pages can contain different words and different numbers of words. To compare them, each page is represented as a vector of word frequencies. We then calculate the cosine similarity between pairs of character vectors.

A value closer to 1 indicates greater similarity between the two text vectors, while a value closer to 0 indicates less similarity.

05 — Interpretation

What does textual similarity mean?

A high textual similarity does not necessarily mean that two characters are directly connected in the Marvel social graph. Instead, it indicates that their Wikipedia pages use similar vocabulary.

Shared storylines

Characters appearing in similar storylines may have pages containing many of the same terms, events and locations.

Shared relationships

Characters belonging to the same teams or interacting with the same characters may have overlapping descriptions.

Similar concepts

Similar powers, identities, affiliations and fictional settings can also produce overlapping vocabulary.

Important distinction: Textual similarity and network similarity are not the same thing. A pair of characters may have highly similar Wikipedia descriptions even if their direct network connection is weak or absent.
06 — Limitations

What should we be careful about?

Word frequency is not meaning

Bag-of-Words captures how often words occur, but it does not understand the deeper meaning or context of a sentence.

Page length matters

Longer Wikipedia pages can contain more words and therefore more opportunities for overlap with other pages.

Wikipedia reflects its editors

Similarity depends on how the pages were written, not only on the actual relationships between Marvel characters.

Simple NLP model

The approach uses word frequencies and cosine similarity rather than semantic embeddings or transformer-based language models.

08 — Conclusion

From social connections to textual connections

Week 5 extends our Marvel network analysis by considering the textual information associated with each character. Using Bag-of-Words representations and cosine similarity, we can quantify how similar different character descriptions are.

The Text Detective game turns this analysis into an interactive experiment, allowing us to test whether humans can predict the relationships that the NLP model discovers.
