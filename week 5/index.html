Behind the game

Turning text into a network question

Each Wikipedia page is treated as a document. We count words and compare the resulting frequency vectors using cosine similarity.

Bag-of-Words

A page is represented by the words it contains and how frequently those words occur. Word order is ignored.

Cosine similarity

Cosine similarity gives a value between 0 and 1 indicating how similar two word frequency vectors are.

Network check

After finding a textual match, we check whether the two characters are also directly connected in the Marvel network.

Text check

What surprised us?

Textual similarity does not necessarily mean a direct network relationship.

Two Marvel characters can have similar Wikipedia pages because they share themes, locations, story arcs, teams, powers, or recurring vocabulary — even when they are not directly connected by an edge in the network.

Limitation

Words are not the same as meaning.

Bag-of-Words ignores word order and deeper semantic meaning. Two pages may use the same words for completely different reasons, while two genuinely related pages may use different vocabulary.

Therefore, the similarity score is a clue — not proof. That is why we inspect the underlying Wikipedia text before interpreting the result.
