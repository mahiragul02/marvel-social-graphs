THE CHALLENGE

Ask One Good Question

This week you learned several different representations of language. You could use TF-IDF, term contrasts, topic mixtures, PPMI context vectors or Word2Vec. But the goal is not to use everything.

CORE PRINCIPLE One good question and one convincing figure beats five methods thrown together.

Whatever you claim, inspect the text underneath it before you believe it.
