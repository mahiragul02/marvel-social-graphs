06 · Interpretation

How should we interpret the results?

Similar vocabulary can indicate similar roles

Characters who appear in similar stories, teams, locations or publication contexts may have similar vocabulary.

Text similarity is different from graph similarity

Two characters can be textually similar without having the same number of network connections.

Shared contexts matter

Characters appearing in the same teams or storylines may have similar descriptions.

Similarity is not causality

High cosine similarity simply means that the pages have similar word distributions.

07 · Limitations

What are the limitations?

Bag of Words ignores word order Word order is not represented in the basic Bag-of-Words approach.
Common vocabulary can dominate General Marvel terms can appear on many pages.
Wikipedia pages vary in length Some characters have substantially longer pages.
Similarity is not semantic understanding The method compares word distributions rather than understanding meaning.

09 · Conclusion

From Connections to Content

Previous weeks examined the Marvel universe as a network of characters and relationships. This week adds another perspective by analysing the text used to describe those characters.

Using Bag-of-Words representations and cosine similarity, we can identify characters whose Wikipedia pages have similar textual patterns.
